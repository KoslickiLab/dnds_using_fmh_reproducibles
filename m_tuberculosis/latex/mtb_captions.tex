\newcommand{\CaptionMtbMain}{\textbf{\method over all pairs of the \mtbAssemblyIsolates{}
assembly-derived \textit{M.\ tuberculosis} isolates, with every \kmer.}
\textbf{(a)} Distribution of the whole-coding-genome value over all \mtbPairs{} pairs, by how closely
the two isolates of a pair are related (sublineages as assigned by \citet{gan2025}), as the share of
the pairs of each kind in bins \mtbHistogramBinWidth{} wide. Dashed line:
\dnds{} $= 1$; \mtbBelowOne\% of pairs fall below it.
\textbf{(b)} The same for each group of genes, over all pairs (box: quartiles; white line: median;
whiskers: 5th and 95th percentiles; dashed line: \dnds{} $= 1$). Diamonds: the per-gene $p_N/p_S$
values \citet{gan2025} report for essential genes, non-essential genes and the PE/PPE family, from a
different estimator, so only their ordering is comparable. Their PE/PPE value was computed over the
high-quality segments of those genes and is drawn beside that group; they report no value for whole
PE/PPE genes.
\textbf{(c)} \method against the conventional estimate (\texttt{seqinr}'s \texttt{kaks}, the function
\citet{gan2025} used), both computed from the same sequences, for the \mtbSeqinrPairs{} pairs of
\mtbSeqinrIsolates{} isolates chosen at random. Grey points: the whole coding genome, one per pair
(\mtbSeqinrPanelOutside{} pairs beyond the axes are not drawn). Circles and bars: for each group of
genes, the median and the quartiles of both estimates, in the colours of (b); the few pairs whose
sequences are identical over a group have no value with either estimate. Dashed line: equality.
\textbf{(d)} Median (line) and quartiles (shaded) of the whole-coding-genome value against the
divergence between the isolates of a pair, over all pairs. Divergence bins holding fewer than
\mtbDivergenceMinPairs{} pairs are not drawn.}

\newcommand{\CaptionMtbScale}{\textbf{What sketching costs when isolates differ at about one site in
several thousand.}
\textbf{(a)} Nucleotide against protein containment for pairs of isolates compared two ways: through
the gene calls of their own draft assemblies (orange; \mtbSmokePairs{} pairs of \mtbSmokeIsolates{}
isolates) and anchored to one reference (blue; \mtbAnchoredPoints{} pairs drawn at random from the
pairs of b). Dashed
line: equal containments. The denominator of the estimate depends on the gap between the two
containments, which nearly vanishes for draft assemblies.
\textbf{(b)} For each group of genes (PE/PPE segments: the high-quality segments of the PE/PPE genes),
the rank correlation between the values from a sketch with the given scale factor and the values from
every \kmer, over the same pairs (a subsample of
\mtbPairedPairsMillion{} million pairs). A sketch with a scale factor above 1 is obtained by
downsampling the scale-factor-1 sketch, which gives the same sketch as sketching at that scale factor.
\textbf{(c)} The median value of each group over all pairs, at each scale factor. Colours as in (b);
dashed line: \dnds{} $= 1$.
\textbf{(d)} The share of pairs in which each ordering reported by \citet{gan2025} holds, at each scale
factor (pairs as in b). Dashed line: 0.5, at which neither group is higher more often than the
other.}
